CYBERLEEK sniper wallets, raw pull hash attestation
===================================================

The four wallets below front-ran the CYBERLEEK launch. Their full raw on-chain
transaction histories are large (tens of MB each) and are archived off-repo on
the analyst's node rather than committed. These SHA-256 hashes let anyone who
obtains a raw dump verify it is the exact data that was analyzed.

Reconciliation with the _summary.json files in this folder:
  The per-wallet summaries were built from an initial working pull capped near
  ~1,200 transactions each. This full re-pull reads every transaction and shows
  higher distinct-token counts (31, 79, 235, and 2,482). That strengthens, and
  does not change, the finding: all four are high-volume, multi-token automated
  sniper / arbitrage bots, not tipped insiders. Each traded CYBERLEEK; several
  round-tripped it to a net near zero, ordinary flip behavior. The 2026-08-18
  front-run buy timestamps (17:48-17:54Z) are documented in the repo README and
  in ../crosscheck/.
